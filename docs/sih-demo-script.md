# SIH Demo Script (3 Minutes)

**Note:** This demonstration relies entirely on strictly synthetic data. No real criminal, police, phone, banking, or personally identifiable data is used in this system.

## 0:00 - 0:30: Introduction & Problem Statement
- **Narrative:** "Law enforcement handles massive amounts of unstructured data. Finding connections manually is slow and error-prone. Our system, the AI-Assisted Criminal Network Analysis System, solves this by extracting networks from text and identifying investigative leads."
- **Action:** Show the login screen.
- **Key Point:** "The system is an investigation-support platform, not an automatic accusation system. It never predicts guilt."

## 0:30 - 1:00: Open Case & Data Extraction
- **Action:** Log in as an Investigator. Navigate to a synthetic demo case.
- **Narrative:** "Here is a case with unstructured synthetic reports. We use NLP to extract entities and relationships. Crucially, we maintain a Human-in-the-Loop approach."
- **Action:** Show the extraction review interface.
- **Key Point:** "Users can accept, reject, or correct extracted information. Every extraction has a traceability link to the source text and a confidence score."

## 1:00 - 1:45: Graph Visualization & Pattern Recognition
- **Action:** Switch to the Network Graph view.
- **Narrative:** "Once verified, connections form a knowledge graph. We can visually explore relationships between synthetic entities."
- **Action:** Highlight an alert or pattern.
- **Key Point:** "The system flags patterns—like frequent communications or shared addresses. These are flagged as *investigative leads* or *priorities*, requiring human verification, not as proof of guilt."

## 1:45 - 2:15: Similarity Search & Analytics
- **Action:** Demonstrate the similarity or analytics pane.
- **Narrative:** "We can find similar entities based on behavioral patterns or connections, helping to cross-reference with other synthetic cases."

## 2:15 - 3:00: Report Export & Limitations
- **Action:** Generate and export a case report.
- **Narrative:** "Finally, investigators can export a comprehensive report that maintains the verification status and source evidence for every claim."
- **Conclusion:** "Our focus is on explainable AI, human oversight, and absolute traceability. Thank you."
