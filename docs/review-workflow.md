# Human-in-the-Loop Review Workflow

As per ethical requirements, AI alerts are **investigative leads**, not automated convictions. Every alert must be reviewed.

## Alert Statuses
- **OPEN:** Newly generated by the AI, pending review.
- **ACCEPTED:** Investigator confirmed the pattern is relevant.
- **REJECTED:** False positive or irrelevant.
- **CORRECTED:** The pattern is relevant but the AI's explanation or extraction was flawed. Requires rationale.
- **NEEDS_MORE_INFORMATION:** Paused until more evidence is ingested. Requires rationale.

## Traceability & Auditing
When an investigator changes an alert's status, the system:
1. Validates the required rationale (for Corrected/Needs Info).
2. Sets `reviewed_at`.
3. Creates a permanent `AuditLog` entry in PostgreSQL capturing the `user_id`, action, and rationale.

This ensures complete accountability for how AI leads influence an investigation.
